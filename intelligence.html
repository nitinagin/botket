Verification methodology

Level 1: Manufacturer claim → Level 2: Documentation reviewed → Level 3: Simulation tested → Level 4: Real-world independently tested. Levels describe evidence, not blanket safety approval. Every future test must specify robot, controller, software version, gripper, task, environment, test method, results and limitations.
