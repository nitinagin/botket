Eight-week sample curriculum

  1. Meet robots: safety and real-world tasks
  2. Build and test movement
  3. Read sensors and collect observations
  4. Use loops and conditions
  5. Navigate a simple obstacle course
  6. Understand AI perception and its limits
  7. Design a team robot mission
  8. Demonstrate, reflect and present

School pilot preview only. No enrollment, teacher certification, kit reservation or payment is available in this demo.
